### 1. In what ways can the emergence of virtual influencers impact the traditional influencer marketing landscape?

Virtual influencers can eliminate human-related risk as there will be no risk of the virtual influencers
speaking or spreading controversial messages without the company consent. Furthermore, virtual influencers
is forever lasting, allowing the influencers to not aged over time. These factor will impact the traditional
influencer marketing landscape as virtual influencer will be seen by the company as a low risk high reward.

consumers. This will undoubtedly make it hard to persuade consumers to purchase the product.

### 3. Can virtual influencers be considered a viable marketing strategy for brands looking to enhance their online presence, and what evidence supports or challenges this idea

Yes, virtual influencers can be a viable marketing strategy for brands looking to enhance their online
presence, but their effectiveness depends heavily on a brand's specific goals, target audience, and the type of
product or service being promoted. Virtual influencers can exist in any location, perform impossible feats,
and wear digital-only clothing, opening up boundless creative possibilities that are not limited by physical
reality. “Imma” have showcases how these digital personas can be used to create visually stunning and
futuristic content that would be difficult or expensive to replicate with human models.
