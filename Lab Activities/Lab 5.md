## Identify the main issues relating to employees and the employer responsibilities in the case study.

The main issues relating to employees in the Volkswagen Diesel Emission Scandal are
employees from various departments, ranging from software engineers, technicians, and managers
were engaged directly in the implementation and concealment of the software that manipulated the
emissions tests. Employees decided to manipulate the tests because of he technology's limitations,
employee pressure to meet the emission rate mandates, and corporate pressure to meet deadlines for
the tests reported.

The main issues relating to employer responsibilities in the Volkswagen Diesel Emission
Scandal are Volkswagen possessed a seriously deficient corporate culture, which focused on taking
care of the demands regardless of means. This in turn cause the upper management failed to put in
place and monitor robust ethics policies and internal controls. Volkswagen is legally and ethically
liable for the wrongful acts of its employees and managers who were acting in the course and scope
of their employment. Management failed to take appropriate action when some of the engineers
voiced concerns about emissions cheating, indicating a breach of the internal reporting structure or
accountability for managers and or supervisors.

## Did the Volkswagen Software Engineers know about the problems associated with their software?

Yes, the Volkswagen Software Engineers know about the problems associated with their
software. Many evidences can proof this statement. The software was not the result of a
malfunction or a defect, but a specific and intentional design that would detect the actual operating
conditions of a laboratory emissions test. While C-level executives denied any direct involvement,
legal filings and investigations revealed that the defeat device was created through an iterative
process that involved many levels of engineers and management. The engineering community was
working under extreme pressure from an autocratic corporate culture to meet reasonable, aggressive
performance and emissions targets. Many accounts suggest that the engineers were in a troubling
moral quandary, they were effectively told to temper the results, rather than to solve the engineering
issue, which effectively would have taken longer and more money.

## What should have been the Software Engineers main concern and responsibilities in this Case Study?

The Software Engineers main concern and responsibilities should have been the public
safety and welfare. They should not have intentionally allowed millions of vehicles to emit nitrogen
oxides up to 35 times the allowed level when driven on the road. Nitrogen is a dangerous pollutant
associated with human respiratory disease and premature death. This shows that the engineering
team valued the financial and performance metrics aligned with the corporation over the health and
safety of society.

Furthermore, software engineers should have a responsibility to act truthfully and be honest
in professional conduct. The software was predicated on intentional systemic dishonesty. To be
honest in all professional statements and conduct, to report accurate data, and not to engage in acts
known to be fraudulent, misleading, or illegal. This shows the failure of an engineer who willfully
wrote software code specifically to deceive official testing equipment as part of a purposeful
scheme for widespread consumer and regulatory humiliation.
