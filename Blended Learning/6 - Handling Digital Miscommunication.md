## Reflection
The netiquette rules I have applied in my response are being polite, respectful, and maintaining
civility, even when a colleague was not being civil to me. You demonstrated accountability for the
delay in getting back to your colleague and offered an action to address their concern. Instead of
being defensive, you were constructive.

The reason a professional tone is so important to digital collaboration is to prevent
misunderstandings from escalating into personal conflict. Professional communication keeps the
team focused on common interests, builds trust among team members, and demonstrates to fellow
team members that management has not ignored them, treating one and another with respect and
recognition that everyone has a voice. One improvement I can make to my own online
communication is to consistently take a moment to pause and re-read a message before sending,
particularly when it is challenging or time-sensitive. This will help me ensure my tone is
constructive and clear.
