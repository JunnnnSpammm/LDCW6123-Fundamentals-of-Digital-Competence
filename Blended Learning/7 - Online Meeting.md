## Identify Issues

1. People talking over each other, turning a meeting into a debate
2. Lack of an agenda and lack of a summary
3. Lack of participation by not opening the camera

## Suggest Improvements

1. Prepare an agenda for the meeting beforehand, and send it to everyone involved in the meeting at least a day before the meeting
2. Appoint a secretary for the meeting to record the minutes of the meeting so everyone can refer back after the meeting ends
3. The meeting will follow the agenda, with only one person appointed for each agenda item. Anyone else who wishes to speak must raise their hand during other people's agenda items

## Draft Meeting Guidelines
1. Everyone will present their report based on the agenda of the meeting, with at least two people agreeing to move on to the next agenda item
2. Use the "raise hand" feature to inform everyone in the meeting that you would like to interrupt
3. Everyone is required to open the camera during the meeting
4. No sending of random and unrelated messages or images in the chat during the meeting
5. The secretary will record the minutes of the meeting

## Reflection
Online meetings often fail because they lack rules and norms when the meeting
is conducted. The three top issues are that people are talking over each other, which
makes the meeting more closely resemble a debate. Additionally, most meetings lack
an agenda and planning beforehand, and no one writes down what is discussed during
the meeting. Lastly, most people don’t open their camera, resulting in a lack of
participation.

I would personally do the following to make online meetings better. First, I
would prepare an agenda for the meeting beforehand and send it to everyone involved
in the meeting at least a day before the meeting. Then, I would appoint a secretary for
the meeting to record the minutes of the meeting so everyone can refer back after the
meeting ends. Lastly, I would ensure that the meeting follows the agenda, with only
one person appointed for each agenda item. Anyone else who wishes to speak must
raise their hand during other people's agenda items.
