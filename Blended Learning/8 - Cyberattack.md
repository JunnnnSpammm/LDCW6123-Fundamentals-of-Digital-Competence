## Type of Cyberattack

Amir fell victim to a phishing attack. It is a type of cyberattack where hackers impersonate legitimate
sources, such as banks or companies, to trick victims into providing sensitive information like login
credentials, credit card numbers, or personal details.

## Digital Assets at Risk
1. Financial information such as the login details for his e-wallet account, which led to the loss of RM2,500.
2. Personal information such as any other personal data Amir might have entered on the fake website, such as his full name, address, or phone number.

## Potential Consequences
1. The loss of RM2,500 from Amir’s e-wallet.
2. The stolen login details and personal information could be used to impersonate him. This could lead to a variety of further problems, such as opening fraudulent accounts in his name or making unauthorized purchases.

## Protective Measures
1. Amir should have checked the sender's email address carefully. Phishing emails often use addresses that look similar to the real one but have subtle differences
2. Amir should have avoided clicking the link in the email. Instead, he could have independently navigated to his bank's official website by typing the URL directly into his browser to verify his account status.
