## Why online reputation management is crucial for personal and business digital presence

Online reputation management is critical because when a customer explores what your business is,
they are really studying a collection of things, such as social profiles, reviews, news stories, and
blog posts, to name a few. And that is normally the very first impression that a customer has about
your business.

First of all, online reputation can drives sales and revenue. Research has demonstrated that
there is a direct association between a business's online reputation and its sales. Businesses that
have a strong, positive reputation are more likely to get new customers and can even charge a
premium for their products or services. On the other hand, a bad online reputation with negative
reviews can drastically cost revenues by deterring potential customers.

In addition, online reputation can impact career opportunities in the future. Employers,
recruiters, and even potential clients will engage in "background checks", and there will generally
be a personal brand presence as they do their research. A strong personal brand provides work
opportunities, business opportunities and partnerships when the opportunity presents itself.
Negative information or outdated and irrelevant information online can slow career growth and
credibility.
