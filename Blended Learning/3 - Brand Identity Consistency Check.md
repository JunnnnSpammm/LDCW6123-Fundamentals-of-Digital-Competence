# Brand Identity Consistency Check - Proton

The Malaysian brand I have chosen is Proton, a Malaysian automotive company that was established on 7 May 1983. After checking their website and 4 of their social media, which are 
their Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok pages, I have the following conclusion:

## Consistency of brand identity in online platform

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8e133733-1211-4b3d-9a28-fe5ac2f226ad">
</p>

They have maintained a consistent brand identity on the online platform. Since 2000, Proton’s logo has featured a tiger. This makes Proton’s logo stand out in the automotive market, 
allowing consumers to instantly recognise the brand. Their logo’s colour had changed from a combination of blue and yellow to a combination of black and white. This is a trend from 
many companies leaning towards simplicity and minimalist designs. While the logo has changed to monochromatic, the gradient of lighting can still be seen in the logo, giving it an 
illusion of reflective metal. The font they’re using for their logo also changed from lowercase letters to capitalised. This is a way to make their brand appear stronger, more memorable,
and more premium. The current logo can be seen on all their social media profile, which would be the first thing netizens see when scrolling through social media, and on the top left 
of their official website. Their logo could also be seen in every single post they make on their social media.

## Customers feedback/reputation in social media

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/31d048d8-37f5-4055-bce2-cfd0b13723eb">
</p>

They manage their customer feedback and their reputation by having a webpage for customers to submit their feedback to Proton. This is the first and fastest way for customers to send
their feedback to Proton, and it will be the first thing that shows up when customers search “Proton Feedback”. Another way to send feedback to Proton is via social media commenting.
While not a lot of customers sent their feedback this way, as can be seen by the low number of comments on their social media post. Posts that have comments show positive feedback
from customers, mainly because of the promotions and sales that the post is showing. It is unknown whether Proton have purposefully removed and deleted negative feedback from their
comments.

## Practical recommendations to strengthen their brand identity in social media

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ef582621-c4c3-4b52-9c95-58afa5508930">
</p>


One practical recommendation I would give to strengthen their brand identity in social media is to remove the titles and slogans from their social media logo. The addition of these
words forces the tiger face to become smaller and can hardly be seen when scrolling through social media. Moreover, in some cases, like on their YouTube page, the titles and slogans
are too long and clip through the circular profile image, making this logo incomplete and uncentered. The tiger face should be enough as their social media profile, as it is already
recognisable by the public. The titles and their slogan would be put in the description of their social media pages. Another recommendation is to get the verified badge on their social
media account. A verified badge is a way to enhance the credibility, visibility, and security of the company. It could also prevent fake accounts from pretending to be the official
Proton pages and scam other customers under the company name.
