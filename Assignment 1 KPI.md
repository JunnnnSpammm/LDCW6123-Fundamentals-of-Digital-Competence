## Interview

### Goal 1
* **Specific**: To raise awareness from the parents about the consequences of gacha game monetization towards children.
* **Measurable**: To reach 5k views for our interview video across all social media platforms, including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook
* **Achievable**: By promoting our interview on Youtube Community, Reddit, Instagram stories
* **Relevant**: To directly address our mission of educating the public about microtransactions and lootboxes by reaching a key demographic (young gamers) on platforms where they are most active.
* **Time-bound**: To achieve the set metrics within a four-month period, starting at the publication date of the interview.

### Goal 2
* **Specific**: To let other victims share their own stories and voices out the consequences so that that public know the true consequences of gambling
* **Measurable**: To reach 100 comments across all social media, including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook
* **Achievable**: By promoting our interview on Youtube Community, Reddit, Instagram stories. Encouraging other victims to sent their stories in the comments section or their own stories with the hashtag #StopGambilng
* **Relevant**: To create a community of gambling victims so that they can help each other and to also show the public how bad can gambling addictions be, especially toward children
* **Time-bound**: To achieve the set metrics within a six-month period, starting at the publication date of the interview.

## Short skit

### Goal 1
* **Specific**: To create a 30-45 second short skit that uses a popular, trending audio or filter from TikTok and Instagram to tell a concise story about the pitfalls of online gambling.
* **Measurable**: To achieve 500 likes for our short skit across all social media platforms, including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook
* **Achievable**: By paying the social media to promote our video to increase visibility, such as Instagram “boost a post” or Google advertisement 
* **Relevant**: To ensure our video get pick up by the social media algorithm and become a viral trend, so that we can show the bad side of gambling to more people under a short amount of time
* **Time-bound**: To achieve the set metrics within a two-weeks period, starting at the publication date of the short skit.

### Goal 2
* **Specific**: To let netizen to sign petition to stop predatory gaming to pressure game developers to not use gambling as a quick way to earn revenues
* **Measurable**: To archive 100 signature on the petition to stop predatory gaming, [https://www.change.org/topic/predatory-gaming-en-us](https://www.change.org/topic/predatory-gaming-en-us)
* **Achievable**: By encouraging viewers of the short skits to click the link at the end of the video to sign the petition, put the petition link in our social media bio, and post description so netizen have easier access to our petition
* **Relevant**: To prevent more and more games using predatory gaming tactic and loot boxes as a way to earn quick money when the large audiences of their game are children who are not educated in the consequences of gambling
* **Time-bound**: To achieve the set metrics within a three-month period, starting at the publication date of the short skit.
